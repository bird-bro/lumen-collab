## Perms-mechanism finding

The house pattern (mirrored exactly) is **`org.springframework.security.access.prepost.PreAuthorize` with `hasAuthority('<perms>')`** — a CLASS-level gate for the page perm (`hasAuthority('patient:rule:list')` on `PatientNumberRuleController`, mirroring `OrgRoomController`/`ExamItemController`) plus METHOD-level button pins on the mutating endpoints. Enablement: framework `SecurityConfig`'s `@EnableGlobalMethodSecurity(prePostEnabled = true)`; the denial envelope: framework `security/handle/AuthDenyAdvice` maps `AccessDeniedException` → `R.ofFail(ERROR_A0301, ...)` over the HTTP-200 transport, while tokenless requests hit `AuthenticationEntryPointImpl` ("203") BEFORE any gate (entry-point precedence — asserted in `PatientPermTests`). `PatientController` intentionally carries NO annotations: authentication-only enforcement falls out of `anyRequest().authenticated()`.
